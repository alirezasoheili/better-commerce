# M1 commerce tracer bullet and recoverable purchase orchestration

The completed M1 architecture closeout chooses Catalog, Pricing, Inventory, Cart, and Orders as the commerce bounded contexts, preserving the installation isolation, module ownership/extraction seams, and outbox guarantees of ADRs 0001-0003. Orders application orchestrates through consumer-owned ports; Order domain and provider modules do not call back into orchestration. Fake Payment is durable infrastructure behind Orders' Payment port, not a Payments context; there is no Checkout/Purchase/Customer/StockLocation/SalesChannel context in M1.

Catalog owns Product aggregates and their Variants/options. VariantId is the stable universal purchasable identity, with immutable Product attachment/selection and creation-time option dimensions. Pricing owns one persisted authoritative installation currency and checked integer-minor-unit calculations. Cart owns anonymous bearer authority, desired quantities/revision, and attempt-bound freeze/unlock/completion. Inventory owns Variant stock and replayable immutable reservations, including cancellation tombstones and ordinary TTL; protection removes a hold from ordinary expiry without introducing payment semantics.

Orders persists an idempotent PurchaseAttempt before module mutation, freezes demand, durably checkpoints one consistent Catalog approval/fact observation before reserve, calculates authoritative prices, and persists an immutable prepared snapshot before protecting stock and creating an Order. Recorded Catalog approval survives later withdrawal; the prepared snapshot is the price cut-off. After successful protection, recovery resolves forward under stable identities. Positive totals create pending-payment Orders and collect; zero totals create paid Orders without Payment. Paid means monetary obligation satisfied; public success additionally requires confirmed stock consumption and Cart completion. Definitive decline cancels before unlock; unresolved payment never becomes decline due to time and keeps protected stock. No transaction spans module/payment calls. Fake Payment commits operation results independently from Order creation and Orders' observed-result commit, making a lost Collected response recoverable without duplicate collection.

Storefront uses Astro with React islands; Admin uses React/Vite and TanStack Router/Query/Table/Form. Both are separate same-origin HTTP/JSON surfaces. Cart secrets persist in a browser map and travel only in explicit bearer headers; JavaScript/XSS exposure and anonymous access loss are accepted for M1, with restrictive CSP and a required transport revisit before real payment/public production. Admin has one installation-level credential, without users/roles/session platform. M1 introduces no durable business events without a concrete consumer; existing events retain M0 outbox guarantees. Customer/shipping/tax/promotions, real gateways/refunds/reconciliation, stock locations/ledger/force-release, and generic policy/workflow/plugin frameworks remain deferred. The [M1 specification](../specs/m1-commerce-tracer-bullet.md) defines implementation/public contracts and acceptance evidence; this ADR records the settled architectural trade-offs rather than reopening them.
